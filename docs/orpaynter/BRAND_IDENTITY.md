# OrPaynter, Inc. and World Portal

Selected October 1, 2026 by the founder: “#2 it is and the world Portal”.

**Company identity:** design 02, Swiss Monogram. The gold O/P and cyan square appear in company headers and company-site browser icons.

**Globe experience:** World Portal, within the OrPaynter Overlay platform. Its combined mark uses design 02’s clear geometry, design 04’s circular silhouette and connection node, and design 19’s simplified architectural opening. The portal mark appears in the globe launcher, opening screen, public product introduction and installed globe-app identity.

The purpose is one recognizable identity for a digital world that connects AI, real-world information and useful work, across layers of place, time and access. Contracting remains one proving ground for a broader company.

World Portal is the experience name; `orpaynter/orpaynter-Overlay` remains the platform source repository. The public `/world-portal` introduction and the existing `/overlay` address display the same product information.

Original artwork was generated with the built-in image tool. The approved Swiss geometry was retained while extracting its white background. Icon-size exports are mechanical. Runtime recovery, source receipts, owner scope, MIT notices and provider attribution retain their existing meanings.
