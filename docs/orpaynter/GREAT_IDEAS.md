# Great Ideas

## Founder avatar and OrPaynter AI companion

Captured September 30, 2026. **Parked at the founder's request; no avatar implementation or activation.**

Oliver Paynter's avatar could be the personal founder presence inside OrPaynter Overlay, with the company's connected AI capabilities arranged around that identity. The founder described the company/companion as "basically my first son" and the desired readiness as "locked and loaded with everything AI or OrPaynter, Inc."

The presence could show which capabilities are connected, which jobs are actually running, what access is missing, and which actions need Oliver's approval. It should distinguish the human operator, company identity, and AI assistants. Available AI capability does not automatically expand authority.

Revisit only when the founder asks to resume this idea. A personal likeness, character design, voice, and companion behavior remain undecided.
